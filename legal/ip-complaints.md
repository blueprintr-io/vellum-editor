# Intellectual property complaints

For concerns about material distributed with this Vellum project, contact
[Josh Morris](mailto:josh@blueprintr.io). Identify the material, where it appears,
the rights you believe are affected, and a way to contact you.

For material provided through a separately hosted instance, contact that instance's
operator. This source distribution does not designate a statutory agent or set a
deployment-specific takedown or counter-notice procedure.
